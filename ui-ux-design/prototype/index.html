The problem

New contributors land on a wall of 200+ listings with no way to tell which ones fit them. 61% of first-time visitors in our research never opened a single bounty. This listing asks you to fix that — for real, on the live product.

What “good” looks like

  • A first-time contributor finds a matching bounty in under 60 seconds.
  • Every listing shows reward, deadline, judging criteria and expected effort up front.
  • Filters that match how people actually choose: skill, time available, reward, region.
  • Designs ship as a clickable prototype, not static screenshots.

Deliverables

  • Clickable prototype (desktop + mobile) of the redesigned discovery flow.
  • Design rationale: 1 page per major decision, with the trade-off stated.
  • Accessibility notes: keyboard path, contrast, focus order, reduced motion.
